Why AI makes this more important

AI compresses the cost of execution. When more people can build, the scarce skills move toward judgment, context, taste, prioritization, and accountability.

AI accelerates the middle.
Designers own the whole loop.

Exploration, prototyping, synthesis, and variations are getting dramatically faster. That gives us more room—not less—to understand the problem and stay accountable for what happens after launch.

What this changes in our reviews

We’ll still care deeply about craft. But a strong review should answer more than “Is this a good design?”

What problem are we actually solving?
Why does this problem matter?
What outcome do we expect?
How will we know if it worked?

Ownership ≠ doing everything

E2E ownership is still cross-functional. PM, RD, Data, UXR, Ops and Design have different responsibilities. The change is that Design actively shapes the conversation and stays accountable for the user outcome—not just the artifact.
